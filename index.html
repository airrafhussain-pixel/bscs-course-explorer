About this project

What this is, and what it isn't.

Why this exists

Googling a course name usually returns an MIT- or Stanford-depth syllabus that has nothing to do with what gets taught in a single semester at UCP. This is a plain-language map of the actual BS Computer Science degree — what each course covers, how many credit hours it carries, and what it requires before you can take it — built so you can decide on a semester or an elective in a few minutes instead of digging through PDFs.

⚠ Disclaimer

This is not an official University of Central Punjab website and isn't published or endorsed by UCP. It's a student-made reference built from UCP's public scheme-of-studies and course-offering documents.

Course structures, prerequisites, credit hours, and — especially — which electives are offered in a given semester all change between intakes and academic years. Always confirm current requirements with your academic advisor or UCP's official student portal before making enrollment decisions.
